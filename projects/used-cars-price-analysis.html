06 · statistical modeling exam project

Used Cars Price Analysis

Type
Statistical modeling exam project
Tools
R
Dataset
171 used vehicles, same make/model
Method
Weighted regression, bootstrap, AIC comparison

Problem / Goal

Identify which factors drive the resale price of used cars, and use the model to predict how price changes as a car ages and accumulates mileage.

What I did

  • Data quality & EDA — checked for missing values and duplicates; scatterplots and a correlation matrix showed a strong negative Age–Price relationship and a weaker Odometer effect
  • Modeling — applied a log transform to the right-skewed Price variable and fit a multiple regression on Age, Odometer, Inspection, ABS, and Sunroof
  • Diagnostics — checked residuals vs. fitted, Q-Q plots, Scale-Location, and Cook's distance/leverage; flagged but kept one influential outlier
  • Heteroscedasticity fix — fit a weighted least squares model; conclusions held versus the original
  • Sensitivity analysis — compared 5 alternative model specifications via AIC, plus bootstrap estimation
  • Prediction — applied the model to 3 car scenarios, back-transformed to euros, and added a depreciation column

Key findings

  • Age is the strongest, most stable predictor: roughly a 1.2% price decrease per additional month, holding other variables constant
  • Odometer has a similar but noisier negative effect; Inspection, ABS, and Sunroof show little to no reliable effect
  • Findings held up under outlier reweighting and multiple alternative model specifications, with the Age effect's confidence interval staying entirely below zero
