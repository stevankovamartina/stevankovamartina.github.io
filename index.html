About

summary

I am an MSc Data Science graduate with a background in International Sales & Marketing, which is probably why I tend to start with the problem before thinking about the model. I enjoy working with data when there is something real to understand, why customers leave, what separates overlapping clinical conditions, or how better reporting can make everyday business processes easier.

Through my studies and experience at Siemens Energy, I have worked with both complex analytical problems and practical business data. What I enjoy most is the part in between: exploring the data, asking the right questions, and finding a way to communicate the result so that it actually makes sense to the people using it.
